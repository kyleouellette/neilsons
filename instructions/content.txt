Specialties

Welcome to Neilson's Sporting Goods, located in Farmingdale, Maine, just three miles south of the state capitol building. The sporting goods store that has been the best known friend to hunters and fishermen since 1949. Owner Gary Hamilton brings a wealth of knowledge in outdoor sporting activities. Gary is no stranger to firearms-check out his Cooper Arms One Shot trophies on display in the showroom. Neilson's has full line of quality new and used firearms, both handgun and longgun, in models and styles that will fit your needs whether it be upland hunting, migratory birds, wild turkey, big game, small game, skeet, target, or personal protection. At Neilson's you'll find an excellent source of reloading supplies including powder, brass, bullets, dies, presses and the necessary tools to get the job done right. Additionally, you'll find an extensive line of blackpowder rifles and accessories including bullets, sabots, powder, and equipment...
